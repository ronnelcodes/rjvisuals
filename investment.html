Ordering and delivery questions

Can I buy just one print?

Yes, provided your physical print order subtotal reaches $20. You may combine sizes and quantities.

How are prints fulfilled?

RJ Visuals reviews your order, checks the crop and image quality, and then places it into production.

Can I download digital images?

Your gallery's permissions and session collection determine whether originals are downloadable. Physical print purchases do not automatically include a digital license.

Is shipping included?

No. Shipping and applicable sales tax are calculated separately by the connected storefront.

Copyright & image licensing

RJ Visuals retains copyright and ownership of its photographs.
